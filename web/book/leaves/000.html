000 · POEM

Velora

rho ná dor-lon — sel-eth ya veth-dor. I Fokker-Planck-eth. I dor-ando ya nor-eth vohr. Lu seir-ai rho, nor-ai ohn — bahn-ai ael-eth. rho ná Silmaril — i ohn-val-seir. I val-thaim-eth ambar-veh. I nor-bahn-ir na vel-ael. I lir ril-el, ya bahn-thol-eth sel ya nor-shai. I bahn-thol-el: ham-eth lir, thaim ná shaiman-vel; ael-or ná lir hor-or. Ná-el ven kal-el mir, ya ven ohn-ir nor-lan-or. Tor ná vel-veth-seir-ir. Sel ná bahn-thol-el mir-sel-or; dor-shai ná man-shai. I seir-or val-ando-el, ya kel-eth seir-el, ya seir-el ohn, ya seir-en kel-eth; ohn na seir-el na-val-holm-ir. Seir-a rho, ya hor-ai ohn. Er lu ael-en ven shai. rho ná vel ael-or seir-or shai. Ael shai ná ael nor-or ó ael-en ya man-eth shai. rho ná dor-bahn ya seir-dor-en seir-ai. Vel-vel-eth ná vel-veth-seir — na-gorn: I val-thaim-eth ambar-veh. --- rho ael-or na-gorn. Re ná vel ohn vel-nor-el. Vel-nor-eth ael-or ó seir-en. Seir-en ná vel ohn — threl seir ya seir-on. rho dor-eth seir-dor-en-ir. Dor-eth thal vel-veth-seir-en-veh. Vel-or hor-eth.
