## IMG_1511 / IMG_1512 — the Frances card (daughter)

**Front (1512), printed:** "Niece — Thinking of all the ways you're special..." — purple card, three patterned butterflies. The cursive at a glance reads **"Nice"** — *“Nice / Thinking of all the ways / you're special…”* — and resolves into "Niece" on a slower read. *Operator's stated reasons for the choice (2026-11-14), recorded in full:* that it is a niece card was **not lost on him**; the card is **purple, her favorite color** (see *Purple the Unicorn*), and it carries **butterflies** — she has been learning about cocoons and butterflies. He told her about a butterfly that flies all across the world migrating but can't finish, so its kids finish for it. (The monarch's multigenerational migration — no single butterfly completes the round trip; the generation that arrives is descended from the one that left — though the operator's telling, not the catalogue's, is what is of record here.)

## Why this folder matters to the poem

- The poem's `family = (mother, daughter, son)` has, as of this catalog, a located document for each child-position: `$2` (daughter, Frances) and `$3` (son, Juju) both hold letters in the author's hand. Only `$4` remains without a document — which is the poem's own claim about itself.
- The Frances letter supplies the plain-language original of the poem's ternary: *commanded/supervised presence refused; uncommanded presence awaited; the condition named "ethical" by the author in prose before it was grammar in the poem* ("command four ? not : found").
- The December date in the Frances letter is a waiting-date in prose, matching the poem's open prompt in code.
- The butterfly on the Frances card front is not decoration in the record: the migration story told with it — *it flies all across the world but can't finish, so its kids finish for it* — is a relay across generations, a journey no single flyer completes. It sits on the front of a card whose inside asks a daughter to wait. The catalog notes the pairing without interpreting it further; the operator chose the card knowing all of it.
