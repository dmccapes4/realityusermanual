## III. The Rebirth Schema

**McClane → Harry Stamper.** Operational reality, rebirthed into a dead astronaut on an asteroid. He does not know he is dead. He files this as pending information. The character is the same; the skin is Armageddon. The math of the straw draw is the anchor memory.

**Feynman → Feynman-who-believes-he-is-Goines.** This is the more complex rebirth.

Jeffrey Goines (Brad Pitt, *Twelve Monkeys*, 1995) is institutionalized for epistemic convictions that the people around him cannot process. He is not wrong. He is — in the film's logic — correct about the nature of the situation: the world is ending, the systems are broken, the institutions are the problem. He has been placed in the institution specifically because his clarity is inconvenient to the systems that house him.

Feynman's animus is epistemic precision. The conviction that if you are not precisely honest about what you know and do not know, you are doing something immoral. The impatience with sloppy language. The insistence on the demonstration over the credential. The van with the equations on it is the public version of this — the assertion that the knowledge is mine, I own it, I will put it on the vehicle I drive.

What happens to that character when the institution decides the conviction itself is the pathology?

The operator's proposal: same person, different skin. Instead of the van and the Nobel and the Caltech position, there is the institution, the diagnosis, and a gap in memory that he cannot account for and cannot confirm was induced. The epistemic approach he knows innately — separate from the memory of the mind, resident in the animus itself — remains intact. He still thinks in exactly the same way. He still knows what he knows. He still cannot stop knowing it.

But now he does not know if his name is Richard or Jeffrey. He does not know if the equations on the van were his or something he read. He does not know if the institution treated him or changed him. He cannot rule out the unethical operation. The evidence available to him is: his own mind, its current contents, and the gap.

## IV. The Dramatic Consequence

The original Feynman functioned as the epistemic check on every claim in the room. He was confident. He was credentialed in his own skin. The certainty came from a life of being proven right often enough to trust the method.

Feynman-as-Goines has the same method and none of the certainty about the self that held it. He still catches the categorical errors. He still knows when an adverb has been applied incorrectly to an irrational number. He still sees the geometry. But he is now also asking, in parallel: *am I the kind of person whose perception of the geometry can be trusted?* Because the institution said no. And he cannot remember enough of the before to refute the institution on its own terms.

This is not paralysis. Goines in the film is not paralyzed. He is relentless. He is simply relentless in an environment that has decided his relentlessness is the symptom, and he does not have the paperwork to prove otherwise.

The operator notes that this will also be interesting from a different angle. The report does not speculate on which angle. The operator will find it.
