## Purpose

This document describes how to eat across a day so the body maintains energy, cognitive throughput, and metabolic resilience without defaulting to convenience.

If iron, vitamin C, and vitamin B12 are absorbed in the right order and rhythm, most other nutritional needs fall into place naturally.

**This is not a purity guide.**  
**It is a regulation guide.**

Meat is not forbidden.  
Meat is contextual.

## The Big Three (Why These First)

### 1. Iron — Energy & Oxygen Transport

- Determines fatigue, stamina, and mental sharpness
- Non-heme iron (plants) requires support and order
- Heme iron (meat) is fast but bypasses regulation

### 2. Vitamin C — Iron's Gatekeeper

- Converts iron into absorbable form
- Protects iron from inhibitors
- Also supports stress response and immunity

### 3. Vitamin B12 — Neural & Cognitive Integrity

- Governs nerve signaling, focus, and mental endurance
- Regulation-heavy nutrient
- Requires explicit attention for vegetarians and vegans

**These three form a regulatory triangle.**  
**If they are aligned, the system stays upright.**

## A Human Day, Walked Through Properly

This assumes a mostly vegetarian baseline, with optional animal intake when depleted.

---

### MORNING (Wake → First Fuel)

**Goal:** Prime absorption pathways. Wake the system gently. Do not overload digestion.

#### Step 1: Hydration + Vitamin C

- Water + citrus (lemon, orange)
- OR berries
- OR bell pepper / kiwi if solid food

**Why:** Vitamin C needs to be present before iron appears.

---

#### Step 2: B12 (Vegetarian/Vegan)

- 25–100 mcg supplement
- Taken away from heavy food

**Why:** B12 absorption is rate-limited and fragile. Morning dosing supports neural clarity and preserves regulation.

**Note:** If you eat eggs or dairy regularly, still supplement lightly. Regulation beats guessing.

---

#### Step 3: Light Protein + Healthy Fat

**Examples:**
- Eggs (if used)
- Greek yogurt / skyr
- Tofu or tempeh
- Nuts + seeds

**Why:** Protein stabilizes blood sugar. Fat slows digestion without blocking absorption.

---

### MID-MORNING → EARLY AFTERNOON (Iron Window)

**Goal:** Absorb iron cleanly, without interference.

#### Step 4: Iron-Rich Plant Foods

**Examples:**
- Spinach, kale, chard
- Lentils, chickpeas
- Pumpkin seeds
- Oats, quinoa

**Must be paired with Vitamin C:**
- Citrus
- Tomatoes
- Peppers
- Strawberries

**Avoid for ~2 hours:**
- Coffee
- Tea
- Calcium supplements
- Heavy dairy

**Why:** Non-heme iron is regulation-heavy. This is the work. This is the training.

---

### AFTERNOON (Sustain, Don't Spike)

**Goal:** Maintain cognitive throughput without depletion.

- Balanced meal
- Protein + complex carbs + fat
- Salt allowed (especially if mentally taxed)

**Salt is not the enemy.**  
**Depletion increases salt demand legitimately.**

#### The Risk of Convenience

- Bypass regulatory checkpoints

---

### EVENING (Restore, Don't Stimulate)

**Goal:** Rebuild without disrupting sleep.

- Warm, mineral-rich food
- Legumes, vegetables, whole grains
- Healthy fats (olive oil, avocado)
- Magnesium-rich foods if possible

**Avoid:**
- Late iron loading
- Late caffeine
- Heavy B12 late at night
