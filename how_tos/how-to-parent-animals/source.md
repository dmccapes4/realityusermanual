## Training Protocol: Entropic Field Remediation

### Step 1: Identify Entropic Field

**In this case:** Poop on floor

**Detection method:** 
- Raptor operator visual scan
- Human baby verbal alert ("poop!")
- Olfactory confirmation (optional, not recommended for raptors)

---

### Step 2: Connect Entropic Source to Entropic Field

**Problem:** Dog created entropy but is spatially separated from consequences (sleeping 5 feet away)

**Solution:** Direct dog's attention to entropic field it created

**Method:**
1. Gentle collar tug (firm, not violent)
2. Guide dog to entropic field (poop)
3. Direct olfactory sensor (nose) to close proximity

**Why this works:** Dogs navigate via olfactory PLEFR (Path of Lower Entropic Field Resistance). Forcing nose near high-entropy signal creates immediate awareness of violation.

---

### Step 3: Provide Immediate, Bounded Correction

**Method:** Light nose tap

**Constraints:**
- Does not cause pain
- Overcomes resistance only
- Bounded intent (clear stop condition)
- Single application (no repetition)
- Firm tone, no yelling

**Purpose:** Associate entropic field creation with immediate, unpleasant (but not harmful) consequence

**Dog's response:** Cowered, retreated to kennel (safe space)

**Assessment:** Correction received, understood, not traumatic

---

### Step 4: Immediate Remediation (Demonstrate Resolution)

**Raptor operator's action:**
1. Clean poop immediately
2. Mop floor
3. Restore order

**Purpose:** 
- Remove entropic field
- Demonstrate correct resolution
- Do not leave dog in prolonged distress state
- Show that entropy can be removed

**Timing:** While dog calms in safe space

---

### Step 5: Restoration Period

**Duration:** Time to make coffee, urinate

**Purpose:**
- Allow dog's nervous system to reset
- Prevent association of correction with prolonged punishment
- Demonstrate that correction is event, not state

**Dog's state during period:** Calming in kennel (safe, correct)

---

### Step 6: Positive Reinforcement (Restoration Complete)

**Method:**
1. Approach kennel
2. Provide food reward (bologna)
3. Verbal affirmation ("good boy")
4. Do NOT pet (dog still processing)
5. Leave dog in peace

**Purpose:**
- Signal correction cycle complete
- Restore positive association with operator
- Reinforce calm behavior in kennel
- Respect dog's need for space

**Critical:** No extended interaction. Restoration, then separation.

## Olfactory PLEFR (Path of Lower Entropic Field Resistance)

### How Dogs Navigate

**Primary sense:** Olfactory (smell)

**Dogs detect:**
- Chemical gradients
- Decay signals (entropy markers)
- Pheromones (social signals)
- Territorial boundaries
- Food sources

**Dogs follow PLEFR via olfactory:**
- Move toward lower-entropy chemical signals
- Avoid high-entropy decay signals (unless investigating)
- Seek familiar scent profiles (safety)
- Mark territory to create known boundaries (reduce uncertainty)

---

### Why Nose-Direction Works

**By forcing dog's nose near poop (high entropy):**
- Overwhelm olfactory system with violation signal
- Create immediate sensory discomfort
- Associate discomfort with entropic field dog created
- Trigger avoidance response

**This is not cruelty. This is using dog's primary navigation system for feedback.**

Human equivalent: Forcing someone to look at mess they made.

**Not supernatural. Just specialized hardware.**

- Emotional states (pheromone changes)

**Collapsed assumption:** "Dogs sense things we can't" → "supernatural"

**Precise statement:** "Dogs have superior olfactory hardware optimized for chemical gradient detection, allowing them to perceive entropic fields (decay, stress, disease) that human sensory systems cannot resolve."

**That's not supernatural. That's just better sensors.**
