## Brief Summary
This is an NSFT companion manuscript that explains human vision as a chain of lossy resolutions from single-photon rhodopsin events through retinal pooling, saccadic erasure, spectral inference, and cortical modeling—not a camera feed. It layers mainstream visual neuroscience with PLEFR/ENAQT framing, prestidigitation timing, and operator-specific optics as a worked example of routing versus detection. Readers leave with a dense theory of what “seeing” is; they only partly leave with how to practice seeing the raw potentials beneath the smoothed percept.
