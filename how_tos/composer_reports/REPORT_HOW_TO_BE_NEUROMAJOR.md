## Brief Summary
This draft field guide targets people with Dabrowskian overexcitabilities who experience sensory and cognitive intensity but reject—or only partially fit—autism-spectrum framing. It mixes operator narrative with channel-by-channel regulation advice, environment routing, endurance under mockery, and building an honest external record. Readers leave with identifiable practices and reframes rather than a fully metered daily protocol.
