## Strongest Contribution
The explicit prohibition against weaponizing dysregulation—triggering a spike then citing the reaction as proof of instability—turns an abstract fairness principle into a recognizable relational failure mode partners can audit in real time.

## What Is Missing
Early-warning observables, default cool-down durations, and re-entry steps after regulation space so partners know when to pause, how long to wait, and how to resume without re-litigating the spike.

## Brief Summary
This artifact is a bilateral, regulation-first relationship guide that applies Dąbrowski overexcitability and developmental-level language to living with a high-intensity nervous system without making diagnostic claims. It tells partners what destabilizes and stabilizes, what overexcitables must own, and how Level III stabilization differs from Level IV internal anchoring, all oriented toward co-regulation rather than winning arguments. Readers leave with lists, one dialogue swap, and ethical guardrails—not a full executable regimen with timing and cues.
