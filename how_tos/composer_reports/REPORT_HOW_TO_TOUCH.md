## Brief Summary
This NSFT companion manuscript argues that contact is never material overlap but gradient exchange between electron fields, classified like flight into four regimes with an ENAQT sweet spot for structure-preserving transfer. It maps hammer, theater fire, ISS needle topology, and Sally Ride’s shoulder tap onto a shared Hoag’s-style cascade and scales the same six-step pattern from atoms to social and scientific reception. Readers leave with a unified theory of contact and a closing imperative; they only partly leave with a repeatable practice beyond “intermediate force, let the gradient work.”
