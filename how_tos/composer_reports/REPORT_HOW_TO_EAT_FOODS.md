## Strongest Contribution
It turns micronutrient timing into a day-walk protocol—C before iron, a guarded iron window, mandatory B12 for plant baselines—under a regulation-first lens that reframes cravings and occasional meat as signals rather than moral failure.

## What Is Missing
It omits daily iron/B12 targets, clock-time anchors for each phase, and a parallel track for readers who already eat meat regularly instead of “mostly vegetarian with optional animal intake.”

## Brief Summary
HOW_TO_EAT_FOODS.md is a canonical rhythm guide for vegetarian-leaning eaters that prioritizes absorption order and timing of iron, vitamin C, and B12 over calorie counting or food purity. It walks one ideal day in steps, explains when and why meat might be chosen, and closes with explicit vegan supplementation rules. The document is as much orientation and de-guilt framing as it is a checklist, but the mid-day iron window is genuinely actionable.
