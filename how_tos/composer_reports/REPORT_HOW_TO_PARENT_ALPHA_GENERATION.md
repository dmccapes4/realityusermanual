## Brief Summary
This is a v0.1 parenting framework that recasts young children as “Alpha Generation navigators” whose meltdowns are structural disorientation rather than misbehavior. It uses a Frances case study to justify calm proximity and self-regulation first, then supplies stepwise guidance for meltdowns, abrupt expectation changes, and refusals. The operative content is practical co-regulation and re-orientation time; the bulk of the file installs vocabulary and negates common misreadings.
