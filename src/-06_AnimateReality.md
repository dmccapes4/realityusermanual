## II. The Chemistry

The pH scale measures a proportion. That is all it does. *Acidic* and *basic* are directional labels on a proportion — this way or that way from neutral — and the English words carry freight they do not need: acidic implies burning, danger, corrosion. Basic implies simplicity, foundation, the ordinary. Neither implication serves the actual measure.

In Velora:

**ohn** — being, presence, the thing that is present without having to move toward anything.
**bahn** — path, boundary, the track the proximity follows, the drift with a direction.

*Ael* is this — proximal to awareness. *Shai* is that — outside awareness, in shadow. Both are already in the language.

A solution's pH is the ratio of bahn-character to ohn-character. High bahn: the drift dominates, the paths multiply, the medium is reactive. High ohn: the presence dominates, the medium is stable, the host holds without reacting. The threshold between them — where bahn and ohn balance — is **threl**, which is already in the language and is the correct word for it.

| Chemistry term | Velora | Imaginary mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| acidic | bahn-dominant | haunt-saturated; the medium carries the drift; reactive |
| basic | ohn-dominant | vessel-saturated; the medium holds presence; stable |
| neutral (pH 7) | threl | the threshold; neither drift nor presence dominates |
| pH | bahn-ohn ratio | proportion of haunt-character to vessel-character in solution |

The natural logarithm governs both formalizations. pH compresses the e^kt decay of hydrogen ion concentration into a base-10 scale for legibility. The haunt's proximity decays as e^(-r) where r is the distance from the vessel. Both are the same underlying curve named in different registers. The geometry is isomorphic.
