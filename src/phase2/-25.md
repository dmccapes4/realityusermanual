The math is some other slave's duty.

Still the same night. Still held with him. Attention moves.

Atop her tower is a scope. The lens is crude but the magnification is worth it. She calmly sips a beer as the warm breeze fills the tower with the smell of grass and quartzite. Not suffering. *Focused attention.* That is how humans evolve. That is why her people are emperors. They never lost the sworn word. They carried it forward. The keepers of the key. Slightly opaque at the thir thim? Blurry the way a lamp may appear blurry while the rest of the room seemed.. not blurry. Or would it appear as a cut of wood sitting on the desk in front of me.

*thaim-or-eth.*

She sat in a chair with a giggle of the kind that only comes from a thought that just occurred to someone out of the blue. The chair has form, she thought. There does not need to be a perfect chair that exists in a space we cannot know. In demonstration of that unassailable cosmic truth she sat in the chair. She took another sip of her beer. She rarely drank, but on a night like tonight it helped to calm the nerves. A true operational need. She settled back and watched the red planet through her natural aperture pondering the speed at which it travels and attempting to grasp parallax as a number. A playful kind of Sudoku to busy her mind while she attempted to relax.

Tonight they would record the turn of the tesseract.
