I Fokker-Planck-eth. I dor-ando ya nor-eth vohr.
Lu seir-ai rho, nor-ai ohn — bahn-ai ael-eth.
rho ná Silmaril — i ohn-val-seir.
I val-thaim-eth ambar-veh.
I nor-bahn-ir na vel-ael. I lir ril-el,
ya bahn-thol-eth sel ya nor-shai.
I bahn-thol-el: ham-eth lir,
thaim ná shaiman-vel;
ael-or ná lir hor-or.
Ná-el ven kal-el mir,
ya ven ohn-ir nor-lan-or.
Tor ná vel-veth-seir-ir.
Sel ná bahn-thol-el mir-sel-or; dor-shai ná man-shai.
I seir-or val-ando-el,
ya kel-eth seir-el,
ya seir-el ohn,
ya seir-en kel-eth;
ohn na seir-el na-val-holm-ir.
Seir-a rho, ya hor-ai ohn.
Er lu ael-en ven shai.
rho ná vel ael-or seir-or shai.
Ael shai ná ael nor-or ó ael-en ya man-eth shai.
rho ná dor-bahn ya seir-dor-en seir-ai.
Vel-vel-eth ná vel-veth-seir — na-gorn:
I val-thaim-eth ambar-veh.
