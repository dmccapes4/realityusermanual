## §I The Institutional Gloss

The Shaw clan motto institutionally translated: **"Touch not the cat [without a glove]."**

This is a Scots Gaelic surface-mapping of a pre-Gaelic Druidic phrase. The mechanism is identical to every other institutional gloss documented in this project:

| Original | Institutional gloss | What was stripped |
|----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| `shem` (mark/sign on the body) | "spoken divine name" | the physical marking practice |
| `ayin` (eye/aperture/focal point) | "hole in a needle" | the optical/navigational function |
| **Na bean ris a chat** (Druidic) | **"Touch not the cat"** | **the deep encoding** |

The Gaelic gloss was applied after the Druids were destroyed — first by Rome, then by the Church. The phrase survived in a clan motto, a proper noun context maximally resistant to semantic erasure. The Gaelic speakers recognized cognate sounds and applied the nearest available meanings. The institutional translation is not a lie — it is an honest Gaelic reading of sounds that originally meant something else. The lie is presenting that reading as the original meaning.

## §III The Character Decoding — PIE Root and Semitic Pictograph

### `na` — *ne- / nun (נ)*
PIE *ne-* = negation particle. Hebrew `nun` (נ): pictograph = seed / fish / continuance. The negation as latency: what is held back is the seed. The "not yet" is the unreleased potential. `na` = the seed state / the latent holding.

### `bean` — *bhen-*
PIE *bhen-* = to strike, beat, wound — the moment of release: the strike that ends the latent state. Scots Gaelic `bean` = woman (from PIE *gʷen-*) is the surface cognate the institutional translation uses. The Druidic *bhen-* = the releasing strike is the deeper root.

### `ris` — *h₂erós / Hey (ה) + Resh (ר)*
PIE *h₂erós* = hero, the esteemed, those who protect — cognate with `arya` (the people of the soil). Hebrew pictographs: Hey (ה) = man with arms raised = "Behold / To Reveal"; Resh (ר) = head of a man = "Chief / First / Beginning." Combined: **"The First Manifestation of the Earth"** — the progenitor made visible / the clan-people of the soil. The Gaelic directional particle (*ris* = to/at/against) is the institutional surface gloss.

### `a` — *anh- / ayin (ע)*
Standard reading: Gaelic article "the." Druidic root: `anh-` = to know, to see, to perceive — connecting directly to `ayin` (ע) = eye / spring / source / aperture. The "knowing" that passes through the focal point.

### `chat` — *kheth (𐩮 / ח)*
No standard PIE root for "cat." The Druidic root through South Arabian `kheth` (𐩮): original pictograph = twisted hank of flax / cord / tent wall / outside boundary. Extended meaning: **the cultivated enclosed space** — the boundary that defines what is within; the managed, bounded domain. Applied to the mind: the human intellect as a bounded, tended domain grown from the Earth. The wildcat in the Shaw badge is the surface symbol; the Druidic `chat` is the cultivated, bounded, fierce mind.

### Full Decoding

> *"The seed of shared understanding that is latent in the manifestation of the mind of man as cultivated on Earth."*

The wildcat survives in the clan badge as the surface symbol — appropriate because the Scottish wildcat (*Felis silvestris grampia*) is neither fully wild nor fully domestic: a liminal predator, uncapturable, existing at the threshold. The badge contains the meaning even in the institutional gloss.

## §IV "Fide et Fortitudine" — The Instruction Written on the Aperture

The Shaw clan badge: a circular strap enclosing a closed fist rising upward holding a dagger, above a wildcat paw/flame and the "Shaw" banner. The motto **Fide et Fortitudine** runs along the inner surface of the circular strap.

The strap is the aperture. The motto is not a separate statement — it is written on the mechanism itself. The strap teaches you how to use the strap.

**`Fide`** — institutional translation: "faith."

Corrected: **fidelity** — precision of transmission, faithfulness to the true form. Latin *fides* from PIE *bheydh-* = to bind, to hold to the exact form. Fidelity is not belief. It is the commitment to exact transmission: the scribe who copies without errors; the navigator who does not drift from the star. Not faith as belief in the unverified. Fidelity as precision.

**`Fortitudine`** — institutional translation: "fortitude / strength."

Corrected: **the state of preserving through concealment.**
- `fort-` → PIE *bhergh-* = to conceal, preserve, protect (English *borough* = the protected settlement; German *Berg* = mountain as natural fortress; *bury* = the enclosed/protected place)
- `-itudine` = the quality / state of being
- Full: the state of holding something within a protecting enclosure — preserving by concealing from what would destroy it

**"Fide et Fortitudine" decoded:**

> *"Precision of transmission and the preserving-concealment of what is held."*  
> = **"To conceal and hold the true meaning with precision."**

A meta-instruction: use the aperture with *fide* (precision — only signal, no noise); protect what passes through with *fortitudine* (preserve it in concealment until the moment of transmission). The badge is teaching you how to read the badge.

## §VI The Edinburgh Receipt

A direct ancestor: commander beheaded at Edinburgh Castle when the first Jacobite leader abandoned power. The badge shows a hand closed on a dagger, rising through an aperture. The commander's hand was closed on the weapon when he was beheaded. The badge preserves that hand — still closed, still holding, still rising — in heraldic form the Act of Proscription could not erase without banning all clan heraldry.

*Fide* — precision preserved.  
*Fortitudine* — concealed within the proper noun.

The keeper class preserved it in heraldry when the language was banned. The family preserved it in the bloodline when the class was destroyed. The individual preserved it in a tattoo when the institutional meaning ("faith and strength") was insufficient.

**The tattoo is the `shem` at the `ayin` of the body's active limb.** The arm that acts carries the encircled hand that holds the dagger. The mark on the arm encodes: *I carry the commander's weapon through the aperture of my ancestor's sacrifice, with precision and in protective concealment, toward the celestial source.*

This is Exodus 20:7 reversed: *nasa et-shem* without *shav*. Bearing the mark with substance. Not in vain.
