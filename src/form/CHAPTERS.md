# Plates for −08

A scene is a run of shots. The wide names the place. The closeups are the frequency that makes the place present. Every prompt is three layers in a fixed order: aesthetic, scene, character. Character sentences come from one lexicon.

Version: `paizo-plate-v2`

## 01 — The Table

### 01a wide

Seed `1508040208`. Cast: feynman, draavio, pyrrhon, njala, merit.

Wide. A round table, seven chairs, one rectangular window, night outside. Five people. A laptop compiling on a sideboard, its screen unreadable.

### 01b coin

Seed `1738501196`. Cast: njala.

### 01c screen

### 01d cards

### 01e links

Seed `3229203761`. Cast: draavio.

### 01f laptop

Seed `3803834808`. Cast: feynman.

Closeup. A laptop on a sideboard, fan implied by the heat-haze of the paint, the screen a blank glow with no readable marks.
