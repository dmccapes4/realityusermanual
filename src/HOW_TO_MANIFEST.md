## I. The Word "Manifest" — Root, Branch, Fruit

### Latin: Caught by the Hand

Latin *manifestus*: manus (hand) + festus (caught, seized). The compound means caught in the hand — caught in the act, undeniable, present to the grip. The cargo manifest is the document of what is on board: what the inspector's hand can already reach. To manifest is to make seizable. A thing that has been manifested is a thing already in someone's hand, or available to be placed there. It cannot be hidden, denied, or lost.

*Manus* (hand): PIE \*men-* — the same root that gives manual, manufacture (made by hand), maniple, manipulate, manage, maintain (main = hand in Old French), manumit (release from the hand — to free a slave was to release them from the master's grip). The hand in this root family is not decorative. It is the agent of grasping, making, releasing. The hand is how the world becomes owned and how ownership is relinquished.

*Festus* (caught): related to *fendere* (to strike, to ward off), *\*ghend-* / *\*ghendh-* (PIE: to seize, to grasp). This root also gives: apprehend (to grasp toward), comprehend (to grasp together — to understand), prehensile, reprehend, impregnable (not seizable). To comprehend is literally to take hold of all of it. A thing you do not yet comprehend is a thing your hand has not closed around.

*Manifestus* is manus + \*ghend-: the hand that seizes. The thing that is manifest is the thing already in the grip. It does not need to be argued for. It is present and grasped.

### The Ship's Manifest

The cargo manifest is the document that makes the ship's contents graspable to the harbor inspector. Not: "the ship probably contains these things." Not: "according to the captain's best recollection." The manifest is the official enumeration — the list that makes the cargo real to the inspector without opening every crate. The manifest is prior to inspection. It is the promise of grasping.

A manifest is a document in which things that exist are made available to the hand. It is the inventory of the present. Not the past (that is a history), not the future (that is a plan), but the present: what is on board, what is in the corpus, what has been gathered and is now enumerable.

### Greek in Matthew

The Greek for "manifest" in Matthew is φανερός (phaneros): visible, evident, shining. From φαίνω (phainō): to shine, to bring to light, to appear. PIE \*bhā-*: to shine, to gleam. This root gives: phenomenon (that which appears), phantom (that which appears without substance), fantasy, fancy, phase (the appearance of a cycle). In Matthew 10:26: "Nothing is hidden that will not be made manifest (φανερωθῇ)." The verb is phaneroo: to make visible, to bring into the light.

The Greek manifest is about light. The Latin manifest is about the hand. Both are about the unavoidable. The ship's manifest makes the cargo available to the hand; the Matthew manifest makes the hidden available to the eye. Comprehend versus perceive. Grip versus gaze.

These are the two modes of knowing: seizing and seeing. A manifest document operates in both: it enumerates (making things graspable) and lists (making things visible). The manifest is the act of bringing into the light and making available to the hand simultaneously.

### What This Means for Method

A manifest is not a record of what happened. That is a log. A manifest is not a plan for what will happen. That is a proposal. A manifest is the declaration of what is present and graspable right now.

**How to build a manifest:**
1. Take inventory — go through the corpus, the ship, the project. Count everything.
2. Enumerate — give each thing a name, a location, a brief description sufficient to distinguish it from everything else.
3. Order — arrange the enumeration by a declared principle. The principle can be alphabetical, chronological, thematic, or — as in the document that follows — by dice.
4. Annotate — add what the inspector needs to understand the entry without opening the crate. Not the full contents; the declared contents. The grip, not the examination.
5. Certify — sign it. A manifest without a declaring agent is a list. A manifest is a sworn enumeration. Here is what is on board. The declaring agent is accountable.
