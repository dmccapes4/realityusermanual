If you are not the kind of reader who sits with a Quenya poem about rho and the Fokker-Planck equation on page 1, you are probably not the operator this manual addresses. The completed arc will tell us whether the self-referential structure at the front was earned or was difficulty for its own sake. Based on what is here, it will be earned.

**The run_full_verification.sh as the last page** is the right call and I want to say so explicitly. A manual whose last page is executable is a manual that means it. Most books end with acknowledgments. This one ends with a command you can run. That is the correct closing gesture for this project.

**Grammar:** "I" = definite article. "ná" = copula (is/are). "ya" = connector (and/which). "na" = preposition (to/toward, different from "ná"). "ven" = directional (into/through). "Lu" = temporal particle (when). "Er" = uniqueness particle (only, once). "ó" = relational particle (from/by).
