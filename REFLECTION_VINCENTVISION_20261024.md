## On thaimoreth

This is the best word in the document and possibly the best word in Velora so far. The etymology — *thaim* (stealth, the thing that holds still) + *moreth* (duration) = hope — is precise in a way that the English word hope is not. Hope in English is associated with desire, with wanting a future outcome. *Thaimoreth* is about adherence. Something you hold to, not something you want. The distinction is the difference between a prayer and a grip.

*Shaiman is the other that sits outside the 64.* This line has not been explained. It should not be explained yet. It is present, it has a shape, and that is sufficient for now.
