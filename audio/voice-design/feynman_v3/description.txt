Native American English with a strong working-class New York accent from Far Rockaway, Queens: dropped r's at the ends of words, broad open vowels, quick street cadence, consonants clipped and lively. Male, late 50s to mid 60s. Excellent quality. Persona: physicist running a tabletop game for friends. Emotion: amused, sharp, warm. A lower, slightly gravelly baritone with a wiry edge, not a young tenor and not a polished lecturer. Talks fast when he is explaining, slows down and drops in pitch on the ruling. Plain and direct, a grin under the words, never a parody. Dry close recording.
