## I. The Four Moves

**Move 1 — Restate the received knowledge.**

**Move 2 — The reversal. The sick made healthy.**
"The speech is not borrowing the restoration as a figure. The restoration is the hinge the
speech turns on." The washing stops the dying. The hand restored. This is not metaphor. It is
the actual pivot of the argument. The healthy form already exists. The specimen demonstrates it.

**Move 3 — Give death to the authority.**
The institution's authorized procedure is what continues the dying. The man the institution
calls crazy stands on the side of the act that stops it. "They leave the synagogue and take
counsel how to destroy him." The speech does not point at that verse. It does the same sequence
of acts.

**Move 4 — Indict the crowd for accepting the authority.**
"There is no right. There is no wrong. There is only popular opinion." The audience is
committed in both senses: committed to the institution, and committed to the space the
institution controls. The speech puts it to them directly that they already believe in the
correct thing, that they accepted the wrong authority anyway.
