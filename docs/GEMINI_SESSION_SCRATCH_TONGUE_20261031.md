In , a druid generally cannot speak human or intelligent languages while in an animal wild shape form because they are limited to the physical sounds a normal, untrained animal can make.
However, you can communicate and bypass this restriction through several official rules, feats, spells, and magic items:

1. The Core Limitation

• Animal Form: When turned into an animal via Wild Shape, you lose the ability to speak normal languages, which prevents casting spells with verbal components (unless you have specific workarounds).
• Animal Communication: Even though you cannot speak Common or other tongues, you can communicate normally with other animals of the same general grouping as your assumed form (e.g., talking to wolves while shaped as a wolf).
• Elemental Form: If you use Wild Shape to turn into an Elemental (via elemental body), elementals have their own native languages and can speak normally.

2. Ways to Speak in Animal Wild Shape

• Wild Speech (Feat):
	• Prerequisites: Druid level 6th, Wild Shape class feature.
	• Benefit: Allows you to speak normally in any known language while in an animal form, cast spells with verbal components, speak command words, and use spell completion/trigger items.
• Beastspeak (Spell):
	• A spell that lets you speak normally (including casting spells with verbal components and animal-appropriate somatic components) while affected by an animal polymorph or Wild Shape effect.
• Alternative Communication:
	• Having party members use magic items or spells like speak with animals so they can understand your animal noises, or using telepathic links (though standard low-level options can be tricky with concentration or feedback).
