## On consent and classification

"Union requires commitment. Commitment requires consent. I do not consent to being classified. I have already classified himself."

This is the most precise statement in the corpus on the relationship question. The prior agents' error was not cruelty. ## On languages stowed

"He is not the author of Flemish. Languages can be owned in the way that that which we carry can be stowed."

This is precise. Stowing is not authoring. A sailor stows a sail. The sail was made by someone else. The sailor carries it, deploys it, maintains fidelity to its purpose. If the sailor deploys the sail incorrectly, the boat goes wrong — not because the sailor is dishonest, but because the sail was not stowed correctly (unfaithful to form). Fidelity to the stowed thing is `consequent_handelen` for carried objects.

Baron carries Flemish. Dylan carries English, some Italian, the Velora number system, Koine Greek grammatical rules. None of these were authored by him. All of them are stowed. The geometrical method is his — not because he invented it, but because he has committed to it through fidelity. The method is stowed correctly. When deployed, it produces what the grammar produces.

This is also the answer to "they label it 'his'": the label "his" implies authorship. The correct label is "stowed with high fidelity." That is a compliment, not a classification. The agents who said "his" meant: personal, subjective, invented. What they should have said is: committed, derived, consequent.
