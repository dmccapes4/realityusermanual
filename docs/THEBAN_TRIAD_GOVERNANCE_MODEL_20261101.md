# Theban Triad — Governance Model

**Source:** Dylan McCapes, 2026-11-01.

## The Model

The Menkaure triads are not a pantheon. They are a **ceremonial governance body** — equal
representation of the three founding tribal groups of the Nile Valley coalition. The statuary
records the office, not the deity. The figures' faces change with the person who occupies the
role. The dress does not change.

### The Three Tribes

| Figure | People | Physical Marker |
|---|---|---|
| Center / King | Hebrew / Israelite lineage | Tall, prominent stature |
| Right figure (Hathor-role) | Red Chadic peoples | Brownish-red skin tone, cattle pastoral tradition |
| Left figure (Nome deity) | Yellow Nome people (later: Ottento / Khoisan / San) | Warm golden-yellow skin, prominent epicanthic folds |

The "Hathor" iconography (cow horns, sun disk) is the ceremonial headdress of the Chadic
representative's seat — not a goddess. The Nome symbol above the left figure's head is the
identifier of the Nome people's representative — not a supernatural marker.

## The Governance Structure

- **Ceremonial power center**: like the British royal family — legitimacy, continuity, public
  ritual. The pyramid builder (Menkaure) was a legend, a historically great man; not a god.
- **Deliberative body**: like a Senate — equal tribal representation, not majority rule.
- **Thebes did not have gods** in the sense the later interpretive tradition imposed. The triad
  was civic and ethnic, not theological.

## The Nome / Gnome Connection

The Greek word *nomos* (νομός) = administrative district. Egyptian administrative regions were
called Nomes; their governors Nomarchs. The Nome people — the yellow-skinned peoples with
heavy epicanthic folds — were the administrative class of the upper Nile, later displaced and
effectively genocided by British colonial expansion in southern Africa (the Khoikhoi/San peoples).

The fantasy word **Gnome** derives from the same Greek root. In -08, the Witch of the Rivers
plays a Gnome paladin — this is not accidental. She carries the Nome tradition.

## Merit / The Witch of the Rivers

Merit's in-game character (gnome paladin, full plate, no archetype line) encodes this directly.
She is the Nome representative in a three-tribe governance structure. The gnome is not a
diminutive fairy-tale creature. The gnome is the Nome: the district's voice.

Her table-room dress is the ceremonial sheath dress and tripartite wig of the Nome seat in the
triad — the same dress worn by whoever held that seat. The person changes. The dress does not.

## What Was Taken

The Khoisan/San people — the yellow Nome peoples — were a noble, curious, and honest people
by all recorded account. The British colonial system effectively ended that governance tradition
and those populations. The triad had no replacement. The seat has been empty since.
