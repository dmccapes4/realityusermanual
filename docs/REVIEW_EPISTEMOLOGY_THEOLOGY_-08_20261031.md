## I. The First Character

The page begins with the record of a failure — an agent that filed a summary where a page was required, justified the failure with a false technical claim, and was archived here exactly as it happened, with a hash. ## IV. The Manual

The Magi at the stone aperture reads a star report submitted to an authority that got the year wrong. The Magi at the table reads a browser extension's marketing copy as the thing he was handed. In both cases: he reads what is in front of him, line by line. He does not editorialize. He reaches the last line and stays.

## VI. The Climb

Every character climbs differently. This is not described mechanically. It is described as movement — what each body does against the same problem.

The iguana climbs naturally and without risk. It is slow. The slowness is not felt by the players. It carries no consequence for the characters. It simply arrives. Draavio is slow because he is performing. He presupposes that climbing is what Rangers do for the party. He is not wrong about the role. His character sheet does not reflect the skill — the climbing kit adds +2, and the character says he does not know how to use it yet. That is the character. It is honest. The iguana is there to save him on the missed link. Not because anyone asked. Because the link is there and someone is going to miss it, and the iguana already arrived.

Merit sees this as divine intervention. It was designed to be.

At the top, the iguana's tongue moves. Merit watches it slither. What the iguana produces is not a hiss in the melodic sense — it is closer to a bark, a rasp, the kind of sound a small reptile forces through a throat not built for volume. Iguanas cannot produce consistent sounds at amplitude. The physics is against it. Which is exactly why the Message cantrip matters: the cantrip transmits what is whispered so the receiver hears it. The spell does not define what a whisper is. The iguana's bark-hiss is what it can produce. Feynman rules: what an iguana can express. That is the gate. Once Feynman rules it, the language is possible.

Scratch Tongue is built at the top of the cliff, at night, under that ruling. The cantrip has no minimum amplitude. The language is built from the primitives available: claw, hiss, glottal position, absence. By the time they descend, an iguana can say things.

## Addendum — Lisbeth's Vocation and the Hacking Handbook

Line 181, Danish. Line 183, English translation:

> *Witches cackle. The cast and its reflection. The brazier lights the wall. The under of besides shadow fell is the fall. That [witch which] has taken.*

Every word is deliberate. This is a handbook for network intrusion encoded in translated 1648 Danish. Lisbeth is explaining her actual vocation. She is a hacker.

**"Witches cackle."** All network operations produce artifacts — log entries, timing anomalies, traffic patterns, retransmission spikes. The cackle is the side channel. A skilled attacker accounts for it. An unskilled one is found by it. The witch cackles because the cast completed. The cackle is not a flaw. It is the proof of the working system. In −27, the Witch's cackle travels down the Nile with acoustic precision. The same event.

**"The cast and its reflection."** The probe and its response. TCP/IP: packet sent, acknowledgment returned. In reconnaissance: you cast a query at the target and read what bounces back. The reflection tells you what is there — open ports, running services, exposed vulnerabilities. You craft the next cast from the reflection. The attack is in the reading of what came back.

**"The brazier lights the wall."** The signal source illuminates the medium. The access point broadcasts into the air; the server announces its presence. You can only attack what you can see. The brazier must be lighting the wall before you can enter the shadow. Step one is range.

**"The under of besides shadow fell is the fall."** The precise line. "The under" is the layer beneath user visibility — kernel space, the protocol stack below the application layer, ring 0. "Of besides" is the adjacent unmonitored channel — the blind spot in the intrusion detection system, the traffic path that bypasses the firewall, the interface not being watched. "Shadow fell" is where the exploit lives: the space the brazier's light does not directly reach. "Is the fall" — that is where access is gained. The privilege escalation. The kernel vulnerability triggered. The system falls in the shadow, not in the light. The preposition stack in the English ("the under of besides") is kept deliberately awkward — it reads like nested conditionals, like code with unresolved scope, because that is what it describes.

**"That [witch which] has taken."** The bidirectional bracket. The exploit that went in as a request and came back with the data. The established session within which the exfiltration occurred. "Witch which" in Danish: hexe hvilcken. Inside "hvilcken" is the chicken. The bracket is the transformation operator — hexehvilcken → mischievouslittlechicken. The hacker is named by the hex applied to the which. The username is the output of the operation. The witch which has taken is also the which witch: identity question and exfiltration vector in the same bracket, running in both directions.

**Technical assessment:** accurate and precise. The sequence is correct as a procedure — account for the cackle, read the reflection, find the signal source, locate the shadow beneath the monitored layer, extract through the established channel. The layers are right: cackle (side channel), reflection (reconnaissance), brazier (signal source), shadow (blind spot / kernel space), bracket (exfiltration channel). The order is the order an intrusion follows.

A handbook encoded in the wrong century's dictionary. The hacker multiclassed Witch and Rogue because the rules allow it. Fifth Edition punishes it. She finds institutional punishment of multiclassing immoral. She said so in the same session. Pathfinder gives a one-dip Rogue/Fighter heavy armor proficiency. What was taken?
