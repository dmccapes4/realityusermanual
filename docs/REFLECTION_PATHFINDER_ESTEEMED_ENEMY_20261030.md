## I. What happened at the real table (measured from his record)

A half-orc Ranger named Draavio was licked by an undead and passed the Fortitude save. Two slam attacks against him missed under a misfortune hex. Undead is his favored enemy. Instead of filing the lick as a checked box, he examined the word *favored*: regarded with partiality; PIE *bha- "to speak"; Latin *favere* "to look upon with goodwill." He settled on **Esteemed Enemy** — estimated with wariness, and invited. Baron laughed. (Baron is a real player at the Friday table; his character is the Druid Njall. Correction 2026-10-30: an earlier draft of this line called him "Baron the Druid.") The GM noted the character had gotten more philosophical. That laugh and that note are receipts from the animate side of the table. They are not proof of anything about −08. They are evidence that the line landed as play, not as a speech pasted onto combat.

Later: a climb he needed aid to finish; a climbing kit the GM would not accept as a "gift"; a vampire gargoyle that demanded something curious, interesting, strange, or beautiful. Draavio fought while defining a single pink carabiner link under those four headings, in order, on his moves. He took about forty damage in a bite/slam/grapple, spent a hero point on the Fortitude re-roll, escaped with help, and the party killed the creature. He put the link on the altar. He found a secret door while checking coffins. In the dark corridor his darkvision made the frame orthogonal without his noticing. His thumb worked a spare link — *click… click… click…* — and taught what he had not yet articulated.

At the back: an outsider crab in a magic circle, eggs in a pyramid, poison under the carapace. The wizard read the bestiary's alignment line. Draavio said evil is a judgment inside a frame. He asked permission to take poison for his arrows. He said, reaching across the circle: **"Normally I rob the dead. It is nicer to steal with permission."** He argued for release on the grounds that an animal cannot sin. The creature objected to *animal* and claimed intelligence. The party resolved to free it and to trap the researchers who had left it there. The freeing was deferred to next session. The trap was designed: presence as pressure, raised stones, rope deformation against a carabiner arm, flintlock *click*, gaseous poison, Sickening Entanglement, Stinking Cloud.

The Adventure Path at that table was Rise of the Runelords. −08 does not use that title. The rooms are semantically similar. That distinction is deliberate.

## II. Why −08 needed this

The first body of −08 (quarantined) was a clean Pathfinder machine: iron golem, admixture, take-20 arithmetic, Vincent taking the villain, the Magi reading the immunity bullets. It was technically careful. It was not yet *measured* against a session that had actually happened. The operator's plan, stated after the fact and consistent with how he uses the book, was always to run the real table as an epistemic playground and then ground the page. Failure and laughter and a rejected gift are datum. Datum accrues into data. The page that only had the geometric distribution of a d20 was still a page about taking. It had not yet paid the average price in world time. Tonight's session was that price.

What the session adds that invention could not honestly supply:

1. **A word examined under contact.** Esteemed Enemy is not a rename for flavor. It is a Fortitude save that did nothing, a lick that did not disgust, and an etymology performed while misfortune ate two slams. The mechanic (*favored enemy*: bonuses on specified checks and on weapon attack and damage vs type — measured, d20pfsrd) stays. The reading of the word is his. −08 now lets Draavio say it at the fictional table before the skeleton finishes falling.

2. **A gift that failed a transactional test and then passed a fourfold one.** The GM rejected the climbing kit. The gargoyle's criteria were still open. Draavio met all four with one link, during combat, in an order that is also a proof: curiosity (clew → summit → knowledge), interest (permit and bind; the rope that wants to be captured), strangeness (geometry that only resolves inside the whole system), beauty (pink refraction as the summit's view; poetry as weight; weight as what the link takes). The wording on −08 is the operator's exact wording, including *Labrynth*, *increased access knowledge*, *The link strange*, *The link beautiful*. Exactness here is not pedantry. He wrote it that way on purpose. A paraphrase would have been a different gift.

3. **Theft restated with permission.** The take-20 query asked whether success by logical inquiry is stealing. The session answered from another angle: taking from the dead is ordinary for him; taking from the living across a boundary with consent is *nicer*. The pink link on the altar is a gift that cost him the object the GM would not price. The vial is a theft the mother agreed to. −08 keeps Feynman's take-20 arithmetic (you pay the average wait; you are given the bad-luck tail) and puts the Archivist's line next to Draavio's. Same question. Two frames.

4. **Amoral nature against a stamped alignment.** The wizard's Knowledge check and the paladin's *detect evil* both return the book's word. Draavio returns ecology. The eggs are a pyramid. The Scarab at Thebes is the same reproach from the other direction (his taxonomy; the Flower / Dancer rules apply when that sky is in play; here it is cited as his parallel, not as a catalogue claim). −08 gives Merit the aura and the sword-down, Sheldon the bestiary line and the concession that "outsider" lacks depth, Draavio the mother and the release. The disagreement is left unresolved on purpose. Next session frees the circle. The page ends on *click*.

5. **The trap as authorship of a boundary.** Presence → pressure → carabiner arm → flintlock click → payload. The circle held the mother in and the researchers out. Inverting who is inside is a change of authorship, not a change of geometry. Sickening Entanglement carries [poison] and sickened (−2 on saves). Stinking Cloud nauseates. The stack is measured from the spell texts. The carabiner-as-fusible-link is his engineering. −08 writes the diagram into the fictional table and leaves Vincent still waiting. He had already begun.

## IV. Meta commentary

Science fiction is the medium. The claims are serious. They are not submitted for peer review; peer review by participants is an honor when it happens. The real table peer-reviewed Draavio without being asked to. Baron laughed. The GM rejected a gift on terms and accepted a trap on terms. Those are falsifications and confirmations of small claims (does abstract ability count as gift; does a poison-descriptor spell ride the wizard's plan). Failure was datum. The pink link was data.

−08 was always about taking: take 20, take a side, take the villain, take the straw on −06. The session makes the verb answerable to a gate you can hold in your hand. A carabiner permits and, in permission, binds. That is also take 20 (you ask; the book already wrote that you could). That is also Esteemed Enemy (you invite what you estimate). That is also the circle (you reach through with permission). That is also the trap (you hold the mechanism in stasis from outside so presence itself completes the circuit).

The orthogonal corridor matters for the book as much as for the Ranger. Half-orc darkvision does not add light. It changes the frame in which the same geometry is complete. Phase 2's characters live in a building that was not there and then was. Draavio does not notice the shift and continues. The click teaches without articulation. −06's Stamper asked whether reading is theft and whether the swordplay sign allows interpretation. −08's Draavio reads a link four ways while compiling a combat, then sets the weight of poetry on an altar that had held coin. The sign under the swords allows swordplay during compilation. The laptop on the sideboard was still on `make -j1`. The Narrator filed that. Nobody else at the table had to.

One caution, labelled: Gemini's replies in the session file praise the play in register that is not this book's. Those praises are not imported into −08. The page uses the operator's words, the rules text, and the Narrator's filing voice. Where I summarize the session here, I am reading his record (measured as "this is what he wrote happened"), not re-validating the etymology of *favor* against a historical linguist's bar. The etymology is his working. The mechanic is Paizo's. Both sit on the sheet.
