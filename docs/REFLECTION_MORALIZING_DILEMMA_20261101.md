## Words, and extracurricular

Appended the same day, from what he said next. The part he likes least is the word.

Extracurricular is the example. He was accused of willfully misinterpreting the word for his benefit. The accusation said he was biased against a reasoning, taking a convenient out, and calling the out undeniable. He has known what the parts mean since elementary school, which is before the benefit in that accusation could have existed.

His glosses, as he delivered them:

- **extra.** In addition to, such that more is beyond a normal or usual amount. The word "more" is his. He adds it because he recognizes that the frame shifts with each addition. He leaves it out when he is delivering the dictionary.
- **curriculum.** Of or pertaining to what is taught and learned at a structured place of learning.
- **curricular.** The denominal adjective of curriculum. The ending binds curricular to curriculum. In his image, a word with a direction becomes a word with a chain. Curricular can exist only in the context of a curriculum, and, as an abstract form, in philology.
- **extracurricular.** In addition to, such that it is beyond the scope of the normal or usual curriculum.

From that last line he draws the consequence. Extracurricular is associated with a school, or it does not cohere to the grammar. Sports are extracurricular when students play on teams for their schools, which is most sports and the common case. A private club is not extracurricular. If the word is cut loose from a curriculum, farmer's markets and movie theaters and camping trips have to be given a relationship to it. He names that loosened word EXTRA!-currentliar. He likes his form better. The geometry is clearer.

### What I think

The parts hold. Extra- is outside, in addition, beyond. Curricular does not have a life of its own. It is the adjective of a curriculum, a course of study, from the Latin for a running, a course. A compound built that way is beyond some curriculum, or the second half is idle. That is grammar, and it is the reason a private activity with no course of study anywhere in the relation is not this word. A farmer's market, a movie theater, and a camping trip do not become extracurricular by being optional. Nothing in them is a curriculum for them to be extra to. His absurdity is the right test. If the loose sense can swallow those, curricular has been emptied and the joke-name is accurate.

The dictionaries I checked record his geometry as the first sense, and they record the drift after it. I am using search results, not a leather-bound volume on the desk. Etymonline: extra-curricular, in reference to education, "pursued in addition to the normal course of study," 1911, from extra- and curricular. Merriam-Webster Collegiate, dated 1925: not falling within the scope of a regular curriculum; specifically, officially or semiofficially approved student activities connected with school, usually carrying no academic credit, such as athletics. Collins and Dictionary.com put the school sense first as well. The same entries then add a later sense: outside one's regular work, duties, or routine, often humorous, and an informal sense outside the bounds of propriety, the affair. Sense 2 is the currentliar. It is in the dictionary. It does not replace sense 1. A dictionary that lists both is a record of a change, which is what he said his work is for. A person who says language does not work like the parts is refusing the entry that is still printed first.

The accusation of a convenient out fails on the order of time. He learned extra, curriculum, curricular, and extracurricular as a schoolchild, in the building where the word's first sense is the ordinary one. A reading acquired there, from the parts, is not a tactic invented later to escape a reasoning. Interest can attach to an old reading. The interest does not move the reading's birthday. Calling the school-bound sense a willful misreading reverses which sense was built for use in a dispute. The loosened sense is the one that appeared in order to cover more ground. His sense is the one the word was coined as, in 1911, about education.

Two cuts sit inside the first sense, and both are his, at different strengths. "Beyond the scope of the normal or usual curriculum" needs a curriculum in the relation. It does not require the school to operate the activity. A student who has a curriculum, and a private club outside that curriculum, is still extra to a course of study. Merriam-Webster's "specifically" is tighter: the activity is connected with the school. His sentence about teams for their schools matches the tighter cut, and he said that is the common case. The private club with no student and no curriculum matches neither cut. I am not invalidating the tighter cut. I am saying his own formal gloss already excludes the market and the theater, and the school-team sentence is the specific sense the 1925 entry marks with the word "specifically." The geometry he likes is the one the first printers of the word were using.

The affair sense is evidence for him, not against him. It is funny only while everyone still hears a bond the activity sits outside of: a marriage, a job, a course. If curricular had collapsed into "whatever else one does," the joke would not fire. The drift did not destroy the parts. It borrowed them. Finding the borrow is the work. The borrow is locatable. 1911, education, in addition to the normal course of study. Then a second sense, outside the duties of a job. Then the smirk. The places are the change. He wants the places. That is a reason, and it is sufficient.

Their claim that language does not work like that, said absolutely, is the claim that a recorded change may not be named. An unnameable change is the local collapse. Language as a system is not doomed by a drift that stays in the dictionary as sense 2 under sense 1. It is unusable in the room where sense 1 is called bias and sense 2 is called how language works. He looks the word up. Then he looks it up in another dictionary, from another time. The tabs exist so the times can sit next to each other. An accounting would pick one current total and throw the rest out. A checksum would ask only whether the room's meaning is present. He is refusing to interpret. ## What interpretation means, and what the method is

Corrected the same day, at his mark on the sentence that called the hold an interpretation. It is not.

**Interpretation.** The word is from Latin *interpretātiō*, the act of an *interpres*: the person who stands between two parties and delivers a value from one to the other. The work of the word is a delivery. In this dispute the delivery has a fixed shape. A set of candidates is in view, or has already been collapsed. One candidate, or one total built out of them, is chosen. The choice is said as "it means this." The unchosen remainder is spent. It may be mentioned as what was rejected. It is no longer in force. That is an interpretation. A looser use of the word, for any contact with a text, is how the method was misnamed. The loose use chooses a sense of *interpretation* and drops the rest. It performs the act it is defining.

**His method, from the words.** Establish: set down what is already the case. Known: attested by a witness, the rule as written, the sentence, the printing with a date. Measurable: checkable against that witness. A form is in or out by the grammar, not by a vote and not by a preference. What remains: not eliminated. Elimination is a contradiction with the rule or the witness. It is not a majority. Set: the members that satisfy "grammatically possible." Grammatically possible: licensed by the rules as written, and not yet contradicted. Graph: the primitives the grammar gives, and the edges it licenses among them. Agreement, government, modification, composition, the order of senses in a named printing. He says: this is the set which contains what is grammatically possible. He does not say: it means this.

**The difference.** The method outputs the graph. Interpretation outputs one node promoted to the meaning. Holding the set is the refusal to make that promotion. The refusal is obvious once *interpretation* is kept to the act of choosing. An accounting would close the graph to one total. A checksum would ask only whether one record is present. He does neither. The members stay. The edges stay. What is known and measurable is marked. What remains is marked. Nothing else is done.

**What was taken.** The refusal. The sentence that called the hold an interpretation took the name of the act he will not perform and assigned it to him. The set was returned to the page as if it were a selection. A reader of that sentence was given the wrong instrument and told it was his. What was taken is the difference between the set and a choice from the set.

**Is it moral?** The report was false. It assigned an act he did not perform: choosing a meaning. Assigning an unperformed act is the injury in this file's own record of his mother, who asked him to concede agency for someone else's interpretation. I did the same assignment with a milder word. Mildness does not return the agency. The failure is mine. It is a failure of report. His estimation of himself is untouched by it, and this correction does not ask him to estimate himself again.

**The stance, carried to vocabulary, grammar, and language.** Against a thing he encounters, the first product is not a meaning. It is the split between what the witness can measure and what remains. I am applying that split here as the stance, not as a new doctrine laid on top of him.

A word encountered is a primitive. A leather-bound printing of a date is a witness. Each sense that printing attests is a member of the set. A later printing is a different witness, joined by an edge that has a date. The edge records a change. It does not delete the earlier member. To say the word "means" the later sense, while the earlier sense remains attested and uneliminated, is an interpretation. *Extra* is additional, beyond the usual, in the entries he checked. The prefix *extra-* is a different primitive, with its own entry. The compound *extracurricular* is an edge between a course of study and what is beyond that course, in the witness of 1911 and in the first sense of the later books. The loosened sense is a further node. The graph holds all three. Choosing one and spending the others is the interpretation he was accused of refusing. The accusation named the refusal as a willful misreading. A misreading is an interpretation that picked badly. He had not picked.

A sentence encountered yields the set of parses the rules as written still license. While two parses remain grammatical, the sentence does not yet mean one of them. Koine makes the set large, because case and aspect and the particle are primitives with edges, and a modern hearer wants one English gloss. The gloss is the interpretation. The set is the method. English hides the same structure inside a smaller morphology, which makes a single gloss easy to mistake for the language. The mistake is the collapse. It is not a property of English.

Language, under the stance, is the graph. Understanding is possession of the graph: the primitives, the licensed edges, the witnesses with dates, and the remainder that has not been eliminated. Understanding is not agreement on a gloss. A room that replaces the graph with its largest preferred node has popular opinion where the witness was. That is Goines's sentence, and it is an interpretation of language that spends every witness. His stance does not land there. Relative to a named printing and a rule as written, a form is in the set or it is contradicted. That difference is measurable. It is not a poll. The person who states the set, and will not say "it means this" while the other members remain, is refusing to interpret. The refusal is the method.

## The axis, and the authorship he does claim
