## The man

Ignaz Semmelweis had the numbers. The maternity ward staffed by doctors who came straight from the autopsy room killed mothers at several times the rate of the ward staffed by midwives. He said: wash your hands. The institution rejected the report. He was committed to an asylum and died there, beaten, in 1865. He was right.

The four moves of the speech that carries his name (from `SEMMELWEIS_SPEECH_IN_THE_QUERY.md`):

1. Restate the received knowledge.
2. The reversal. The sick made healthy.
3. Give death to the authority.
4. Indict the crowd for accepting the authority.

−08 performs all four across the whole session, and adds a fifth. The operator laid them out on 2026-11-07. This document records them in order, with the lines.

## 1. Restate the received knowledge

**The note, read as evidence (1497).** Raymora the Dragon, delivered by Joey, reads Draavio's note: "I have to go see about a goat." Subject: I. The probability that someone else wrote it and Draavio merely filed it "is very low." It is the received knowledge stated with full forensic seriousness about a goat. That is the humor and that is the move: state the plain fact as if it needed proving, because in an institution it does.

**The restatement redeemed as note-taking (1500–1514).** Lisbeth: Draavio would have taken a note there, and the Narrator would have narrated it. Feynman, quietly: he would. Lisbeth: without his meticulous note-taking the fabric of the party dissolves. The record-keeper is named as the thing holding the party together. Semmelweis's whole case was a record kept carefully.

**What is a union? What defines commitment? (1517–1532).** Merit answers that a code offers bounded freedom of movement, and that more often it is "performance of guilt driven by fear dressed as honor." Sheldon answers that commitment is in consistent action: "You learn by paying attention."

**Joey delivers the handwashing (1535–1538).** In character as Raymora: "You don't wash your hands on a schedule for Treat Deadly Wounds… You wash your hands on a schedule for Long Term Care. The stethoscope isn't theater." That is Semmelweis's finding stated as ward procedure, from the doctor at the table. Then, as Joey, its antecedent in the nature of civilization: the yard as *yad*, palm down on the soil; consent as the measure of commitment; one code only, "do not police your neighbors." The Witch adds the strike over delayed pay, "not by malice, just bureaucracy," and Lisbeth gives Draavio's answer: bureaucracy is amoral because nature is amoral.

**The party speaks in rhythm (1550–1610).** Morality is a measure. Measures require frame. Frames are a just geometry. Geometry is just expression. Each player takes one line in turn and each line hands off to the next. Overlapping roles working together: doctors and nurses, doctors and midwives (`REVIEW_-08_CLIMAX_20261101.md` line 20). They already knew how to do it. The trap was performance.

Lisbeth: *Forpligtelse kræver ikke forberedelse.* Commitment does not require preparation.
Vincent: Preparation is what commitment allows.

The crowd agrees. Feynman does not tell Vincent not to talk. Then Feynman calls the break (1612). The operator: "That is serendipity."

## 2. The sick made healthy

**The apple in the tree (1480).** In *The Sunless Citadel* the Gulthias Tree bears one red apple a year that heals. The iguana sits in the oak on the healer's perch, saw Draavio leave, cannot say so in Scratch, and will not burn the Wild Shape it needs for later. He nods. No one is paying attention. The healing is waiting in the tree.

**The thrice-kindled redeemed by his own gift (2153–2201).** Sheldon is about to convert his fire to electricity against the golem. The Magi says "Don't," turns Sheldon's notes the right way up, and puts a finger under the line Sheldon wrote and underlined twice: fire slows a flesh golem. "I was never without access! I simply had not read the bullets!" The gift was meticulous note-taking. It was his own. He had forgotten it.

**The gift of language allows redemption (2260).** The iguana drops from the oak and opens the extract with the same claws that took the rope and built Scratch on a cliff in the dark. A language made at the top of a mountain is what lets the doctor act at the bottom of the sinkhole.

**The note-taker and the doctor who swears the word forward (2565–2568).** Draavio checks the inside of the coffins: no scratches, no one is coming back. Raymora: "Please take a seat and stay calm. I will return immediately when I am able." He says it to the golem the way a doctor says it to the one patient who must not get up. He keeps it.

**Allowed to cease (2572–2604).** The flesh golem sits beside the oak, partially in the ground, in the roots of the redeemed Gulthias Tree. It likes sitting. It hadn't before. The doctor returns, the iguana crawls the body, and the claws cut the stitches at just the right speed. It ceases. The forty-six hit points stay.

## 3. Give death to the authority

**The paladin delivered divine judgement, and it was recorded (2595).** Forty-six: the number Merit's sword left. Nothing in the conversation took one. The record keeps it.

**The wraps were always wet (2601–2604).** Sheldon to the Witch: the wraps were wet, otherwise the glucose would not accept the natron lye; there were no resins. The Witch: "It has always been so." The consensus position (dry wrapping) is the authorized procedure. The operator: "That is the only way the chemistry works lol." The received account dies to the mechanism. Nobody has to argue with it; the dirt already contains what the derivation describes.

**The Archivist chapter flips it (2608–2891).** Act 10 is this move run inverted, many ways at once. The figure the card names as villain keeps the record and convicts no one. "The trial does not convict. It measures." The one the bestiaries bound in the desert for teaching is the one asking each party what they took. The party declines to hand him the villain's role, the goat carries the verse out, and "Is it theft to read?" is answered "Only in frames that charge for it." Semmelweis was killed for an accurate report. Here the keeper of the accurate report is the authority in the room, and he uses it to ask, not to punish.

## 3/4. The researchers

**They represent academia, and they mirror the party minus Draavio (1866, 2882).** Four researchers: one reads first and acts second (Pyrrhon), one is small and always in front (Njála), one speaks before she has to (Merit), one has a healer's kit open on her belt (Raymora). When the pen opens, "the researchers, still sick on the stones, do not object; they could not if they wanted to," and the goats go up into the last of the light. The party is looking at itself as an institution, sick, and is not asked to condemn it.

## 4. Indict the crowd → BEWARE, and gospel

**Lines 2904–2931.** Nature is amoral: "Without it the whole system becomes a story someone is telling about you, and you spend the night fighting the narrator instead of the golem." Divine alignment is with immutable cosmic law, not a faction. The magical beauty of nature is revealed through the underlying mechanics, not the outer shell. "The warning is not against seeing it. The warning is against stopping there."

Then the party says one sentence together, run backward so the beginning arrives last: *Beware. Not the outer shell of perception…*

This is Move 4 without a scapegoat. In the Semmelweis speech the crowd is indicted for accepting the authority. Here the indictment is a warning spoken by the crowd itself, in unison, about the habit that made the authority possible: reading the first line and stopping. It is gospel in the plain sense: the news, told by the people it is about.

## 5. The sworn word, sworn forward

**Lines 2934–3002.** Swearing the sworn word forward brings higher alignment with cosmic law. The rest is labels and orders in a bind. Presence is what the checksum counts.

- Vincent: "I took the whole thing. I simply took it in a different frame."
- The *Gattaca* certificate: heart disorder, 99% probability, life expectancy 30.2 years. "The label was real. The 30.2 years was not." He flew.
- Sheldon: "The label is only the floor if you stand on it."
- Lisbeth: *Han overtog sin egen journefart.* He took charge of his own departure.
- Feynman on the unnumbered page: the crisis was a missing label, not a missing number; the order was already there.
- The trap, back in Act 8: "It measured presence. That is the only honest thing a trap can do."

The doctor's "I will return immediately" is the sworn word. He returns. That is the checksum: not the label on the patient, not the order on the certificate, but whether the one who swore is present when it is time.

**The epilogue (3008–3019).** The operator truly commits. Vincent's Paizo account request is filed from the operator's own address: "Anyway, I would like to become a User." *Committed* carries both senses the Semmelweis speech gives it: committed to the work, and committed to the room the institution controls. Semmelweis was committed in the second sense for keeping the first. The word is for those committed, in Semmelweis and in the Bible.

## The doctor at the table

Joey plays Raymora the Dragon, who is Doctor Drake. He writes DOCTOR in the margin where the sheet has no box. He washes on a schedule for long-term care, carries the stethoscope because it makes pressure legible, gives the golem his word, and keeps it. `REVIEW_-08_CLIMAX_20261101.md` line 20: the character represents what doctors could be if doctors and midwives worked together as equals with overlapping roles. The party scene in Act 7 is that arrangement, already working, before anyone planned it.

The Magi, Merlin, is Semmelweis expressed (`REVIEW_-08_CLIMAX_20261101.md` §XII): the scholar killed for an accurate star report, counting the sounds of a golem made from scholars like him, and stopping when there is nothing left to write.

Two Semmelweis figures, then: one who is killed for the number, and one who washes his hands and is believed. −08 puts both at the same table.
